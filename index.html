Your personal fitness & nutrition coach

Fitness & Nutrition is a mobile app that builds a workout plan and a meal plan around your goal, then helps you follow it day by day.

What the app does

Personalized plans

Set a goal (lose fat, gain muscle, stay fit) and get a weekly workout schedule and daily calorie and macro targets.

Meal planning

Hundreds of recipes with nutrition facts, arranged into daily meal plans that match your targets.

Guided workouts

Exercise library with animated demonstrations, step-by-step instructions and on-device rep counting.

Progress tracking

Log weight, body measurements, meals and workouts, and see your progress over time.

Contact & support

Questions, feedback or account requests? Email us at support@fitness-support.online. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Emails sent by the app (such as password-reset codes) come from no-reply@fitness-support.online and are only sent to users who request them.
