Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Fitness & Nutrition mobile app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google.
  • Profile information you provide: age, sex, height, weight, body measurements, fitness goal, activity level and dietary preferences.
  • Activity data: workouts, meals and progress entries you log in the app.
  • Health Connect data (optional): see "Android Health Connect" below.
  • Crash and error reports: when the app hits an error, it sends a technical report (error message, app version, device model and Android version) to our server so we can fix the problem. These reports do not include your photos or camera images.

Camera: the rep-counting feature processes camera frames entirely on your device. Camera images and video are not uploaded or stored by us.

Android Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, the app asks for permission to read the following data types: steps, distance, active calories burned, heart rate and exercise sessions. The app does not write any data to Health Connect.

This data is used only to show your daily activity, count your active minutes and workouts recorded by other apps or wearables, and adjust your daily calorie target to your real activity level. Health Connect data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except the service providers listed in section 3 that store it for us. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect or in your device settings.

The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and adjust your workout and meal plans.
  • To show your progress and history.
  • To send transactional emails you request, such as password-reset codes.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data, show third-party ads, or send marketing emails.

3. Storage and sharing

Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. Transactional emails are delivered through Amazon Simple Email Service (SES). We share data only with these service providers, as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app (Settings > Delete account), or by emailing support@fitness-support.online; we will complete the request within 30 days.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to our systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

7. Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness and nutrition information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact support@fitness-support.online.
