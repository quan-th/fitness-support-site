Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Fitness & Nutrition mobile app for Android and iOS ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who processes it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who is responsible

The app is developed and operated by Steve Tran, an independent developer, who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: support@fitness-support.online.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. If you sign in with Apple (iOS, where available), we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name.
  • Profile information you provide: age, sex, height, weight, body measurements, fitness goal, activity level and dietary preferences.
  • Logs you keep in the app: meals, workouts, water intake and progress entries.
  • Health data (optional, only with your permission): activity data read from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. See section 4.
  • Sign-in sessions: the model name of each device you sign in on (for example "Galaxy Note 9"), so you can see and sign out your devices in the app.
  • Crash and error reports: when the app hits an error, it sends a technical report (the error message, a technical stack trace, the operating system — Android or iOS — and the time) to our server so we can fix the problem. Reports do not include your photos or camera images.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier or browsing history.

3. Camera and photos

  • Camera: the camera is used to take progress photos and to analyse your exercise posture (rep counting and form check). Posture analysis runs entirely on your device; camera frames are not uploaded and are not stored.
  • Progress photos: stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to our server. If you choose to export a photo to your photo library, that is an action you start yourself, and the copy is then managed by your device.
  • Photos you pick for a form check: analysed on your device only and not uploaded.

4. Health data

Apple Health (iOS)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app asks for permission to read the following data: steps, walking and running distance, active energy burned, exercise minutes, heart rate, and workouts (walks, runs and hikes recorded by other apps or an Apple Watch). The app does not write any data to Apple Health.

This data is synced to our server on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and is used only to show your daily activity and to adjust your daily calorie target to your real activity level. Data from Apple Health is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold, is not used for data mining, and is not shared with third parties except the service providers in section 6 that store it for us. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app (your profile > Apps > Fitness & Nutrition) or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, the app asks for permission to read the following data types: steps, distance, active calories burned, heart rate and exercise sessions. The app does not write any data to Health Connect.

This data is synced to our server on AWS and is used only to show your daily activity, count your active minutes and workouts recorded by other apps or wearables, and adjust your daily calorie target to your real activity level. Health Connect data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except the service providers in section 6 that store it for us. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect or in your device settings.

The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and adjust your workout and meal plans.
  • To show your progress, activity and history.
  • To send transactional emails you request, such as password-reset codes.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

No tracking, no ads, no sale of data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not show ads, do not sell or rent your personal data, do not send marketing emails, and do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

6. Service providers and where data is stored

We share data only with the following service providers, as needed to run the app, or when required by law:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosting, database and storage. Your data is stored in the AWS Asia Pacific (Singapore) region.
  • Amazon Simple Email Service (SES): delivery of transactional emails such as password-reset codes.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, only if you choose it.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, only if you choose it (iOS, where available).

7. International transfers

Our servers are in Singapore. If you use the app from another country, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your data is transferred to and processed in Singapore. Where the law requires it, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses included in our providers' data processing terms.

8. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

  • Contract: account, profile and log data, to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Consent: health data from Apple Health or Health Connect, which you can withdraw at any time by revoking access (see section 4).
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports and sign-in sessions, to keep the app secure and working.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose data by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete and export (port) your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. How to use them:

  • Access and correct: view and edit your profile and logs directly in the app.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app (see section 10).
  • Export, object, restrict or any other request: email support@fitness-support.online from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, goals, plans, logs and synced health data from our server, and the data and progress photos stored on your device. The original data in Apple Health or Health Connect is not affected. You can also request deletion by emailing support@fitness-support.online; we will complete the request within 30 days.

11. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as salted hashes, data is encrypted at rest on AWS, and access to our systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

12. Age requirement

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness and nutrition information. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified professional. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

15. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact Steve Tran at support@fitness-support.online.
